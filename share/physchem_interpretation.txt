1. READ THIS FIRST
------------------

These are computed descriptors, not predictions. Nothing in this analysis is
fitted, trained, or correlated against the experimental outcomes. The observed
conditions and results travel in the same table so a person can look at both
at once; no relationship between them has been calculated, and none is
implied.

The sample is 10 binders, of which 2 did not express, 3 went to the pellet and
5 to the supernatant. That is far too small to support any conclusion about
which properties matter for expression or solubility. No p-value or
correlation coefficient against an outcome column has been computed, and none
should be. Treat the group comparisons in section 6 as a check that the
pipeline produces sane numbers, not as a result.

Two of the descriptors deserve specific scepticism. The instability index and
the aliphatic index are literature heuristics derived from contexts unlike
recombinant expression in E. coli; they are not quality scores and a value
either side of a conventional threshold means little here.

2. WHAT WAS COMPUTED
--------------------

From amino-acid sequence alone: length, molecular weight, isoelectric point,
net charge at pH 7.0 and 8.0, GRAVY hydropathy, instability and aliphatic
indices, aromaticity with explicit F/W/Y counts, cysteine count and parity,
the full 20-residue composition, charged fraction over DEKR, reduced and
oxidised extinction coefficients at 280 nm, Shannon entropy of composition,
the longest run of AVILMFWYC, and the maximum mean Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy
over a 9-residue window with its position.

The CSV carries 21 headline descriptors per row plus the experimental
metadata. The complete table, including the per-residue composition, is the
file physchem_properties.tsv in run directory
2026-11-05T0754Z_physchem_descriptors-10binders.

No structural information is used. A structural tier (solvent accessibility,
secondary structure) and a protein-language-model embedding tier are planned
but not implemented, so nothing here accounts for how the chain actually
folds.

3. VERIFICATION
---------------

The implementation was checked against independently published values before
being run on these sequences: 66 checks, 0 failures (run
2026-11-05T0749Z_physchem_smoketest). The reference protein was bovine-
ubiquitin-P0CG53-1-76, for which the published values are molecular weight
8564.84 Da (matched within 0.5 Da), isoelectric point 6.56 (matched within 0.2
pH units, the tolerance being there because the pKa table used here differs
slightly from the ExPASy web implementation), and extinction coefficient 1490
M-1 cm-1 (matched exactly).

Edge cases covered a 5-residue peptide, sequences with no cysteine and no
aromatics (where the extinction coefficient must be exactly zero), an all-
alanine sequence, and rejection of sequences containing non-standard
characters.

4. TWO ROWS PER BINDER: READ THE RIGHT ONE
------------------------------------------

Purification tags and expression leaders change every number in the table, and
this set mixes tagged and untagged constructs. Each binder therefore appears
twice, distinguished by the construct_form column:

  as_given      the construct as supplied, i.e. the protein that was actually
                expressed. Use this to interpret an observed experimental
                outcome.

  tag_stripped  the binder with terminal tags removed. Use this to compare
                binders with each other, because otherwise the comparison is
                partly between tags.

The stripped_elements column records exactly what was removed from each
sequence; nothing was modified without being recorded. Stripping removes a
terminal run of at least 6 histidines at either end, a leading MGSS, MGS or M
leader, and a GS-rich linker only where it directly abuts a removed tag.

This is not a cosmetic difference. Removing IL10NB1's histidine tag moves its
isoelectric point from 6.63 to 5.09, because six histidines are a real
titratable group near neutral pH.

IL10NB5 is deliberately left intact: it is a two-domain construct whose halves
are joined by GGGGS repeats. Those linkers are structural, not purification
tags, and are retained (stripped_elements = none).

8. KNOWN DEFECTS IN THE INPUT DATA
----------------------------------

LCN2_B1 carries the literal value CONFLICT in its host_strain and iptg_mM
columns, because the source sheets disagree. This has been carried through as-
is rather than guessed at. Any analysis touching those two fields must handle
it; the descriptors themselves are computed from sequence and are unaffected.

The data_quality_flag and source_sheets columns record provenance per row and
are worth reading before using any outcome column.

A literal NA in soluble_fraction or elisa_result means not applicable or not
measured, and is distinct from an empty cell. These values are written out
exactly as recorded, so a spreadsheet that silently converts NA to blank on
import will lose that distinction.
